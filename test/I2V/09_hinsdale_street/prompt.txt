A quiet residential street in Hinsdale, Illinois extends beside a straight concrete sidewalk on a clear day. Mature deciduous trees cast broad canopies over trimmed lawns, flower beds, driveways, and detached houses with pale siding, porches, pitched roofs, and front steps. Several dark sedans and lighter vehicles are parked along the curb and farther down the road. Hedges, ornamental shrubs, young trees, mailboxes, and narrow strips of grass organize the space between homes and pavement. The sidewalk, parked cars, repeated houses, tree-lined verges, and distant continuation of the neighborhood remain clearly connected as a complete suburban streetscape.
