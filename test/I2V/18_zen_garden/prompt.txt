A photorealistic Japanese zen garden is organized around broad beds of fine white gravel raked into precise concentric curves and flowing parallel lines. A tall stacked-stone cairn rises near the center among smooth gray boulders, clipped moss mounds, low shrubs, sculpted pine trees, and a traditional stone lantern. A still pond occupies one side of the garden, bordered by rocks and dense green planting. A tall bamboo fence encloses the grounds while additional trees and layered vegetation fill the perimeter. The raked patterns, stone arrangements, water, moss, lantern, and carefully maintained plant forms remain balanced as one complete contemplative landscape.
