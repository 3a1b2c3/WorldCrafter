An active volcano rises above a barren field of black basalt, cooled lava, coarse ash, and deeply rutted ground. A vivid stream of molten lava descends from the crater and continues across the lower slope, while a dense white-and-gray plume expands above the summit. Jagged rock formations and low fractured ridges break up the foreground, leading toward broad ash-covered flanks and distant dry mountains. The open blue sky contrasts with the dark mineral terrain and red-orange lava. Crater, smoke column, lava channels, volcanic rock, traversable ground, and surrounding ridgelines remain clearly connected as one complete geological scene defined by exposed terrain and active volcanic processes.
