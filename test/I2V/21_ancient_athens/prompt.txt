An ancient Athenian temple stands at the end of a broad stone-paved approach within an open archaeological landscape. Weathered Doric columns support a carved entablature and fragmented pediment, while missing roof sections reveal the interior shadow and sky beyond. Uneven marble slabs, low foundations, scattered blocks, retaining edges, and partially preserved walls spread across the surrounding ruins. Sparse dark trees and low vegetation rise between the pale stone structures, with distant hills and additional archaeological remains extending across the horizon. The temple, processional path, broken masonry, open ground, and wider Acropolis setting remain equally legible as one continuous historic environment.
